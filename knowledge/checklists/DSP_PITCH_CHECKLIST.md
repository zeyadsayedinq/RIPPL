# DSP Pitch Checklist

**Hard rule: submitted no later than 4 weeks before release date.** Editorial
consideration effectively closes after that. This checklist exists because the
late pitch is the single most common cause of a failed release.

## Prerequisites
- [ ] Audio delivered to distributor and accepted
- [ ] ISRC + UPC assigned and recorded in `/releases`
- [ ] Split sheet signed and stored in `/vault`
- [ ] Samples/interpolations cleared in writing
- [ ] Artwork final, 3000×3000, no logos that violate DSP rules

## The pitch itself
- [ ] Primary genre + secondary genre chosen deliberately (not "Pop" by default)
- [ ] Mood and style tags selected
- [ ] Instruments listed
- [ ] Language of the vocal specified
- [ ] Culture / territory tags where relevant (e.g. Egyptian, Arabic-language)
- [ ] Recording location and date
- [ ] 500-character pitch written: what the track is, who it's for, why now
- [ ] Comparable artists named (3, realistic — not aspirational)
- [ ] Marketing plan summarised: spend, creator count, TV/radio, tour dates
- [ ] Any prior traction quantified (previous release D28, save rate, socials)

## Beyond the DSP form
- [ ] Anghami / regional DSP pitch submitted separately
- [ ] YouTube Art Track / Content-ID confirmed
- [ ] Editorial playlist curators contacted directly where a relationship exists
- [ ] Independent playlist outreach list built in `/creators`
- [ ] Artist profile: bio, header, "artist pick" updated *before* pitching

## After submitting
- [ ] Pitch date logged in `/calendar`
- [ ] Do not change the release date after pitching — it voids consideration
- [ ] Follow-up reminder at T−2 weeks for regional DSPs
