# Revenue Models

Seven lines RIPPL can run. Diversification here is not ideology — it is cashflow
timing management (see `foundations/MUSIC_MARKET_STRUCTURES.md`).

| # | Line | Cash speed | Margin | Ceiling | Effort to start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recorded streaming | Slow | High | Very high | Medium |
| 2 | Publishing | Very slow | High | High | Low (admin) |
| 3 | Sync licensing | Medium | Very high | High | Medium |
| 4 | Brand / creator deals | Medium | High | Medium | Medium |
| 5 | Commissions & services | Fast | Medium | Low–medium | Low |
| 6 | Live & ticketing | Fast | Low–medium | Medium | High |
| 7 | Affiliate / referral | Medium | Very high | Low | Very low |

## Sequencing for a new operation

1. **Start with 5 and 7.** Fast cash, near-zero setup, funds everything else.
2. **Add 1 and 2.** The compounding asset. Slow, but it is the actual business.
3. **Add 3 and 4** once catalog and relationships exist.
4. **Add 6** only when there is an audience to sell to — live is the most
   capital-intensive and least forgiving line.

## Subscription / SaaS layer

RIPPL itself can be a product (`/billing`): tiered plans with credit-metered AI
actions, modelled on `ha346/AI-Saas-Platform` (Stripe subscriptions, free tier
with API limiting). Treat it as line 8 — highest margin, highest build cost,
longest payback.

## Portfolio rule

No line above 50% of forecast revenue. At least one line paying inside 30 days.
Review the mix quarterly in `/dashboard`.

## Checklist

- [ ] At least two lines active
- [ ] One line pays inside 30 days
- [ ] Mix reviewed quarterly
- [ ] Each line has a named owner
