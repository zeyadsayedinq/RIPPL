# Unit Economics

Per-unit maths for each RIPPL revenue line. Fill the assumption cells with your
own numbers; the structure is what matters.

## Recorded release

```
Revenue     = streams × net_rate_per_stream × your_master_share
Cost        = production + mastering + artwork + distribution + media_spend
Break-even  = Cost ÷ (net_rate × master_share)
```

At a `[ASSUMPTION]` net rate of $0.003/stream and a 100% master share, every
$1,000 of cost needs ~333,000 streams to break even. This single calculation
should precede every production budget conversation.

## Custom / commission work

```
Margin per job = price − (hours × your_hourly_floor) − licence/sample costs
Effective rate = margin ÷ total hours including admin, revisions and chasing payment
```

Most producers price on studio hours and lose the margin in admin and revisions.
Track *total* hours for three jobs and you will re-price permanently.

## Live show

```
Net = (tickets_sold × price × your_split) − (fee_guarantee_shortfall + travel + crew + backline)
```

The break-even ticket count belongs in `/live` before the show is confirmed. If
you can't state it, don't confirm the date.

## Creator / UGC campaign

```
Cost per qualified view = (creative_cost + media_spend) ÷ views_at_3s
```

Not cost per view. Views under 3 seconds have no measurable downstream effect.

## Affiliate

```
Contribution = referred_revenue × (1 − commission_rate) − attribution_tooling_cost
```

Affiliate is the only line where a higher payout rate can be strictly correct —
the marginal cost of a referred sale is near zero.

## Hourly floor

```
hourly_floor = (target_annual_income + fixed_costs) ÷ (billable_hours_per_year)
```

Billable hours are realistically 40–55% of worked hours for a solo operator.
Using 100% is the most common pricing error in music services.

## Checklist

- [ ] Break-even stream count computed before production spend
- [ ] Total (not studio) hours tracked on at least 3 jobs
- [ ] Break-even ticket count stated before confirming a show
- [ ] Hourly floor recalculated annually
