# Music Market Structures

## Where the money actually sits

| Layer | Who pays | Latency | RIPPL surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recorded streaming | DSPs → distributor → you | 60–120 days | `/releases`, `/dashboard` |
| Publishing / mechanical | PRO / MRO | 6–18 months | `/vault` |
| Sync | Brand, film, game | 30–90 days | `/invoices` sync pipeline |
| Brand / creator deals | Brand directly | 30–60 days | `/roster` deal sorter |
| Live & ticketing | Promoter / box office | Settlement night → 30 days | `/live` |
| Services (mix, custom, commission) | Client | On delivery | `/invoices` |
| Affiliate / referral | Partner platform | 30–60 days | `/affiliates` |

**Structural fact:** the fastest-paying layers (services, live, affiliate) are the
ones that fund the slowest-paying layer (recorded). Most independent operations
die of cashflow timing, not of low revenue.

## Attention market

Discovery is search-and-feed driven. Two distinct motions:

- **Search intent** — "type beat", "artist name type", playlist-name searches.
  Compounding, low volatility, rewards catalog depth.
- **Feed serendipity** — TikTok/Reels/Shorts. Spiky, high ceiling, zero memory.

Do not fund a feed strategy with the expectation of search-like compounding.
Feed buys spikes; catalog buys a floor.

## Platform dependency risk

Rank every revenue line by *single-point-of-failure*:

| Line | Dependency | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Streaming | 2–3 DSPs | Own the email list; direct-to-fan |
| UGC virality | 1 algorithm | Cross-post; repurpose to owned channels |
| Sync | Agent relationship | Multiple agents; direct brand outreach |
| Live | Promoter | Own ticketing (`/live`) for at least small rooms |

## Checklist

- [ ] No single platform is >50% of forecast revenue
- [ ] At least one line pays within 30 days
- [ ] Owned audience (email/SMS) grows every month
