# A&R and Artist Development

## Theory

A&R is portfolio management under extreme uncertainty. Most signings fail; a small
number carry the book. Three implications:

1. **Optionality beats conviction.** Cheap early-stage options (singles deals,
   development agreements) outperform expensive late-stage certainty.
2. **You are buying a trajectory, not a snapshot.** Absolute follower count is
   nearly useless; 90-day slope, save-rate and repeat-listener share are not.
3. **The artist's own operating capacity is a signal.** Artists who ship on time
   without being chased convert marketing spend far better.

## The five signals worth tracking

| Signal | Where RIPPL shows it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 90-day follower slope | `/roster` artist card, `/dashboard` | Momentum, not size |
| Save / listen ratio | Soundcharts sync → `/dashboard` | Intent to return |
| Repeat-listener share | DSP for-artists | Ceiling estimate |
| Content cadence held ≥ 6 weeks | `/studio`, `/campaigns` | Operating capacity |
| Audio-feature fit vs target playlist | `/hitlab` Hit Score | Placement realism |

## Stage gates

RIPPL's scouting Kanban (`Discovered → Evaluating → Negotiating → Signed`) should
be gated, not vibes-based. Do not advance a card until:

- **Discovered → Evaluating** — 3 tracks listened end-to-end; slope pulled; one
  competitor artist named.
- **Evaluating → Negotiating** — Hit Score run on 2+ tracks; a written 1-page
  thesis in `/studio` scratchpad; budget band agreed internally.
- **Negotiating → Signed** — term sheet in `/vault`; split sheet drafted;
  release window blocked in `/calendar`.

## Development plan template (90 days)

| Weeks | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Positioning | One sentence: "X is the artist for Y who want Z" |
| 3–4 | Catalog audit | Which 3 tracks carry the campaign; kill the rest |
| 5–8 | Content system | 12 UGC hooks live, 3 posted per week |
| 9–10 | Pitch | DSP pitch submitted ≥ 4 weeks pre-release |
| 11–12 | Release + review | Post-mortem written into `knowledge/` |

## Checklist

- [ ] Written thesis exists before money is spent
- [ ] Trajectory (not follower count) drove the decision
- [ ] Hit Score run and *disagreed with explicitly* if overridden
- [ ] Split sheet drafted before the first session, not after
- [ ] Exit / renegotiation trigger written into the deal
