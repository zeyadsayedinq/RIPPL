# Content Pillars

Four pillars. Every post belongs to exactly one. If it belongs to none, don't post it.

| Pillar | Share | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proof** | 30% | Show the work is good | Live take, studio moment, before/after mix |
| **Process** | 30% | Build parasocial trust | "How this beat started", session teardown |
| **Point of view** | 25% | Make you findable and memorable | Opinion on the scene, taste-making, reactions |
| **Offer** | 15% | Ask for something | Pre-save, ticket link, custom work, merch |

## Why the split

The failure mode of artist accounts is 90% Offer, 10% everything else — an account
nobody follows. The failure mode of "content creator" artist accounts is 0% Offer —
an audience that never converts. 15% Offer is the smallest number that still
trains an audience to expect asks.

## Mapping to the release cycle

| Phase | Proof | Process | POV | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −8 to −4 weeks | ▲ heavy | ▲ heavy | normal | none |
| −3 to −1 weeks | normal | ▲ heavy | normal | pre-save |
| Release week | ▲ heavy | light | normal | ▲ heavy |
| +1 to +4 weeks | normal | normal | ▲ heavy | light |

## Checklist

- [ ] Every post tagged with a pillar in `/studio`
- [ ] No week is more than 25% Offer
- [ ] POV content exists — it's the pillar most often skipped
