# Sync & Brand Deal Pipeline

Fast-paying revenue that funds slow-paying recorded revenue. Runs through
`/roster` (deal sorter) and `/invoices`.

## Stages

`Sourced → Pitched → Briefed → Shortlisted → Negotiating → Contracted → Delivered → Paid`

Move a deal only on evidence (a reply, a brief, a signed doc). "They seemed
interested" is not a stage change.

## What must be true before you pitch

1. **Clean splits.** One unresolved writer share kills a sync in due diligence.
   Split sheet in `/vault` first — always.
2. **Instrumental + stems exist.** Most syncs need an instrumental within 48h.
3. **One-sheet ready.** `/releases` one-pager PDF, plus a 30s edit.
4. **Rights statement.** Who controls master, who controls publishing, in writing.

## Pricing bands (orientation only, negotiate per use)

| Use | Typical driver | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Social/organic brand post | Follower reach, term | Lowest band, fastest close |
| Paid social campaign | Territory + term + media spend | Term length dominates price |
| TV / broadcast spot | Territory, term, exclusivity | Exclusivity is the big multiplier |
| Film / series | Scene prominence, festival vs wide | Often MFN'd against other cues |
| Game | Perpetuity is common | Push for a buyout floor |

`[ASSUMPTION]` — bands vary hugely by territory and rights-holder. Always get a
comparable before quoting.

## Negotiation levers, cheapest to most expensive to give

1. Term length → 2. Territory → 3. Media types → 4. Exclusivity → 5. Perpetuity

Give term before you give exclusivity. Never give perpetuity plus exclusivity in
the same deal without a materially higher fee.

## Checklist

- [ ] Split sheet signed before pitching
- [ ] Instrumental + stems ready

- [ ] Deal logged in `/roster` deal sorter with a real stage
